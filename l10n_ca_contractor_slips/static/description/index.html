Prepare T4A box 048 and T5018 contractor tax slips in Odoo 18, using the vendor payments recorded in your accounting. Review each contractor's total, trace the bills and payments behind it, and create a CRA XML file for your business to review and upload.

The module covers two Canadian contractor reporting workflows:

  • T4A, box 048: fees for services paid to self-employed people and businesses, before GST/HST and PST, for each recipient paid more than $500 in the calendar year.
  • T5018: payments to construction subcontractors, GST/HST and PST included, for the reporting period (calendar or fiscal year). By default a slip is issued when the services part, tax included, reaches $500 (Form T5018SUM); a setting applies the slip page's test of more than $500 before tax instead.

Amounts come from what you actually paid. The module reads posted vendor bills and the cash matched to them, so a bill dated in December and paid in January counts in January, a refund lowers the total. T4A leaves goods out; T5018 follows the selected mixed-bill setting. A credit note applied to a bill counts as settling both, each at its own mix of services and goods. Foreign-currency bills count the Canadian dollars actually paid, and an early payment discount reduces only the bill that earned it. Every amount on a slip links back to the bills and payments behind it.

From there you can:

  • review and adjust slips (each adjustment needs a reason and is logged),
  • finalize the return, which produces the CRA XML file and checks it against CRA's published 2027 schemas before you upload it,
  • print customized slips laid out to follow CRA's rules for slips you print yourself (Information Circular IC97-2R20), two copies per sheet, email them to recipients who agreed to electronic slips, and let recipients download their own slip from the portal,
  • amend a filed return or cancel a slip, with the report codes CRA expects, and give recipients a copy marked amended or cancelled.

It runs on Odoo Community and Enterprise, on Odoo.sh or your own server, and uses standard Odoo records throughout: contacts, the Tax ID field, account tags, vendor bills, payments and bank reconciliation. Odoo Online databases cannot install modules from the app store. The module downloads CRA's public XML schemas from canada.ca the first time you finalize a return.

Questions about T4A and T5018 in Odoo

Which T4A slips does this module prepare?

It prepares T4A box 048, fees for services, from supported contractor payments in Odoo. It does not prepare the full range of T4A income boxes or employee T4 payroll slips. Review the setup instructions and known limits with the person responsible for your return.

Does it submit the return to CRA?

You download the T4A or T5018 XML file and upload it through CRA's Internet File Transfer service using your own authorized account. The module checks XML structure against the published schema. It does not submit the return or verify CRA acceptance.

Can I trace an amount back to the vendor payments?

Each calculated slip lists the supported payments and bills behind its amount. Review these lines, the return's warnings and any adjustments before filing. Opening balances and journal settlements need the checks described in the setup instructions and known limits.

How do recipients get their copies?

You can print recipient PDFs or provide them through the recipient's Odoo portal. Email delivery requires the recipient's consent and your own configured outgoing mail server. Review portal access, consent, paper requests and delivery results before treating delivery as complete.

Can I install it on Odoo Online?

Use Odoo 18 Community or Enterprise on Odoo.sh or your own server. Odoo Online does not allow installation of this custom Python module. The accounting workflow uses Canadian localization and the setup described below.

What does the beta cost?

The module is free under LGPL-3. Hosting, Odoo subscriptions and any separately agreed implementation or support services are outside that price. Check each slip against your records before filing. Your business remains responsible for the return and recipient delivery.

Who should maintain the annual filing setup?

Assign an owner to check the current CRA schema, form requirements and your reporting settings before each filing season. Read the known issues and roadmap before choosing a filing workflow. Current CRA interpretation questions remain part of the beta review.

Before you file

This module helps you prepare T4A and T5018 returns for the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA). It gives no tax, accounting or legal advice; decide what is reportable with your accountant.

You remain responsible for every slip, the file you upload, deadlines, delivery to recipients, and any CRA penalty or interest. This is a beta release, so check each slip against your records before filing.

Schema validation checks the file's format only. It cannot tell you whether amounts or identifiers are correct, or whether CRA will accept the return.

Privacy

The business using this module is accountable for the personal information in it under PIPEDA or its provincial equivalent. Solvync receives none of it. The only outside call the module makes downloads CRA's public schemas from canada.ca, and it sends no data.

SINs are collected under section 237 of the Income Tax Act and used for these slips only. Odoo stores them in the database and its backups without encryption, so give accounting administrators two-step login and mask SINs in any copy made for support. Each SIN sits on the contact, kept for the main company that collected it, on every slip that reported it and in the CRA file stored with each return. Clearing it from the contact leaves those copies, and the module cannot yet remove SINs from final or filed returns, so agree with your accountant how you will destroy them once each return's six-year record period ends. CRA requires the recipient's consent before slips are emailed, and emailed slips carry the full SIN over ordinary email. Odoo deletes the emailed copy once it is sent; a failed email keeps its PDF until the slip is emailed again, for at most 30 days. Odoo 18 controls dynamic template editing through system administration and Mail Template Editor permissions. Give those rights only to people trusted to see SINs; Odoo 18 has no separate Restrict Template Rendering setting. An exposed SIN will usually meet the real risk of significant harm test, which brings duties to report and notify (PIPEDA section 10.1; Alberta PIPA section 34.1).

Uninstalling the module deletes every return, slip and CRA file it holds, any slip email still waiting, and the SINs, slip settings and email consents stored on contacts. Download the CRA files, keep the PDFs you need and export your consent records first. The T4A and T5018 tags are removed from your accounts as well.

Configuration

Work in the main company for all of the steps below. Returns belong to the main company, cover every branch, and read their settings, each contact's slip type and each contact's email consent from there. In a database with several businesses, each main company files its own returns, with its own program accounts, slip types and email consent. Odoo shows the settings to administrators only.

On Odoo 18, the module also installs the standard VAT Number Validation module for Canadian business-number checks. Preparing and merging contractor contacts needs Odoo's Contact Creation permission as well as the accounting administrator role. Grant these through the normal user settings.

  1. In Accounting > Configuration > Settings (Invoicing on Odoo Community), under Canadian Contractor Slips, enter:

    • the payer's RP program account (T4A) and RZ program account (T5018), for example 123456789RP0001;
    • how you sign in to CRA's Internet File Transfer: My Business Account, a web access code, or Represent a Client. With My Business Account, enter your 9-digit business number or a 15-character program account as the transmitter account. With a web access code, the file carries the 15-character account you sign in with (the return's RP or RZ account unless you enter another). A representative enters their RepID and, if they like, the firm's name as transmitter name; the RepID goes in the file only with Represent a Client;
    • the filing contact, a person with a 10-digit phone number and an email address of at most 60 characters;
    • the T5018 reporting period: calendar year or fiscal year. Once you file for a period, CRA expects the same period every year unless it authorizes a change in writing;
    • whether to report the whole mixed bill on T5018 (the default) or the services lines only, and which T5018 threshold to apply (see the setting's help).

    File T5018 only if more than half of your business income comes from construction. Other payers report construction subcontractors in T4A box 048. Slip amounts must be in Canadian dollars, so the company's currency must be CAD.

  2. On each contractor's contact, in the Invoicing tab, set Canadian slip to T4A or T5018 and choose the slip recipient type: individual or sole proprietor, corporation, or partnership. A return will not finalize until it is set on every contractor. Enter the SIN for individuals while working in the main company. Each main company keeps the SIN it collected, visible to its own accounting administrators only. The surname and given name come from the contact name; type them on the contact when the name has more than two words or is written surname first. The return lists each slip's surname and given name, so check them before finalizing. Businesses use the standard Tax ID field for their 15-character business number. Tick Slips by email only when the recipient has agreed, in writing or electronically, to receive slips by email, and keep that consent with your records. Consent covers only the company it was given to, so a recipient paid by two of your businesses consents to each separately. Tick Wants paper slips for anyone who asked for paper. The other recipient details (name, address, recipient type) belong to the contact, so every company that can open the contact sees them. Where businesses in one database should not see each other's contractors, set the contact's Company field to the main company that pays them. Avoid setting it to a branch: head-office users without that branch could not open the contact. Merging a contractor's contact with another moves its SIN, consent, slip type and slips, filed ones included, to the kept contact, whose portal login can then open them. So only an accounting administrator who works in every main company that pays the contractor can merge it. Duplicate rules set to merge automatically in Odoo Enterprise's Data Cleaning run as the system and skip that check, so leave contractors to manual merging. Each vendor bill records the contact's slip type when it is posted, in the Canadian slip field under Other Info. A contractor who moves from T4A to T5018 mid-year keeps the earlier bills on T4A. Bills posted before you flagged a contact follow the contact. An accounting administrator can set a bill to Not reported to leave it out.

  3. Tag the expense accounts you pay contractors from with T4A box 048: fees for services or T5018: construction services. Bill lines on those accounts count for contacts set to the slip, and the return names any other vendor paid on them. For T4A, lines with a service product count too. Lines with a goods product never count. If you hold back part of each subcontractor bill, tag the holdback account with the T5018 tag as well, so holdbacks count when they are paid. Payments booked from bank, cash and credit card journals straight to a tagged account count as well. T4A payers who hold back part of a contractor's fees tag the holdback account with the T4A tag. Release a holdback with a vendor bill on the holdback account, or pay it straight from that account; a payment that settles a journal entry is flagged and left out, because the entry does not say what it paid for. The same goes for opening balances: in your first year on Odoo, enter unpaid contractor bills as vendor bills on a tagged account so their payments count.

    Odoo copies the tags of the account whose code sorts just before a new account's code. After you tag an account, check the tags on any account you create next to it, and review which accounts carry each tag before every filing season.

    CRA asks for box 048 on payments between businesses, including corporations, in every industry. It currently assesses penalties for leaving box 048 out only where its administrative policy for trucking applies: from 2025, businesses in the trucking industry paying Canadian-controlled private corporations in that industry. That policy concerns completing box 048; penalties for filing an information return late are a separate matter. Flag service contractors unless your accountant advises otherwise.

  4. The CRA schemas download automatically the first time you finalize a return. To fetch them ahead of time, use Download CRA schemas in the same settings block. A server without internet access can point the system parameter l10n_ca_contractor_slips.cra_schema_url at a copy of CRA's schema package on an internal web server.

  5. Print slips at actual size, with any "fit to page" or scaling option in the print dialog turned off: CRA's rules for slips you print yourself set their size and text size.

Stored CRA XML and queued slip PDFs are private to the paying company's accounting administrators. Public attachment flags and download tokens do not grant access to these files. Upgrading the module revokes legacy public links and restores payer restrictions while retaining the file bytes. Recipients use their own slip portal, which applies the recipient and masking rules.

Usage

  1. Go to Accounting > Reporting > Contractor Slips (T4A, T5018) (Invoicing on Odoo Community) and create a return on the main company: choose the slip type and the year. A T4A return covers the calendar year. For T5018 on a fiscal year, the year is the one in which the period ends. The return shows its due date and notes a period that has not ended yet, or a gap since the last T5018 period.
  2. Click Calculate slips. Each slip shows the amount calculated from payments, the payments behind it, and any warning: a missing SIN or business number, an incomplete address, a name split from the contact, characters CRA will not keep, a payment not matched to a posted bill, or a payment that settles a journal entry. The return also names vendors paid on tagged accounts whose contact is not set to this slip, and contractors whose slip for the period was cancelled in an amendment; the notes are kept in the return's messages when you finalize. Contacts with an address outside Canada are left out and listed on the return; confirm each one is a non-resident before filing.
  3. Correct the contacts and source records and click Recalculate, or enter an adjustment with its reason. Adjustments survive recalculation. The recipient details on a slip come from the contact, so correct them there. Payments made outside Odoo belong in Odoo too, for example as a bank entry to a tagged account: the $500 test uses the calculated amount, so an adjustment cannot create a slip for someone below it.
  4. Click Finalize. The module refuses to finalize if bills, payments or contacts changed since the last calculation, checks the settings and every slip, builds the XML file and validates it against CRA's schemas. Download the file and upload it through CRA's Internet File Transfer, then click Mark as filed and record the confirmation number.
  5. Deliver the slips by the due date:
    • Email slips queues an email for each recipient who consented, sent within a few minutes by a scheduled job, and never sends the same slip twice. Each slip shows whether its email is queued, sent or failed, with the reason for a failure, and the return's messages name each recipient whose email failed so you can print their slip or email it again. Sent means your outgoing mail server accepted the email. A message that bounces after that shows only in the sender's mailbox, so give a paper copy to anyone who says the email did not arrive.
    • Recipients with a portal login they have used find their slips under Tax slips on their portal home page.
    • Print slips prints two copies of each slip on one sheet for everyone else, including recipients who asked for paper, whose email address is not valid, or whose portal login was revoked or never used; the summary after emailing names them. Print duplicates prints replacement copies marked DUPLICATE. Giving T5018 slips to subcontractors is optional.
  6. To correct a filed return, open the latest filed version and click Amend. Change the slips that are wrong and explain the change in the amendment note, naming each recipient (up to 1,309 characters), then finalize. Only changed slips go in the file; a change of address alone does not amend a slip. To cancel slips, tick Cancel this slip in a separate amendment, because CRA takes amended and cancelled slips in separate submissions. Recipients of a cancelled slip get a copy marked cancelled by email, on the portal and in print. Contractors missed on the original go in a new original return for the same year, which picks up only the contractors the filed return does not have.
  7. Keep a record of when and how you asked each individual for their SIN, of each recipient's email consent, and of how each slip reached its recipient.

Known issues / Roadmap

  • Quebec RL-1 slips (box O, code RD) are not produced. Revenu Québec requires software certification for RL-1 XML.
  • No T4A box 022. If you withheld income tax from a contractor, which is rare, CRA needs a slip for them whatever the amount: set their bills to Not reported so this return leaves them out, and file their slip separately, for example with CRA's Web Forms.
  • Each amended submission totals and counts only the slips in it, as CRA's corrections page describes. Confirm with your accountant before filing your first amendment.
  • To correct a SIN or business number already filed, cancel the slip in an amendment and file it again in a new original return. CRA's pages do not say how an amended slip with a different identifier is matched to the original; the module warns when an amended slip carries a new one.
  • The federal identity signature on printed slips is drawn from the public-domain flag beside the agency's name. CRA's own artwork is the exact reference.
  • A contact counts as a non-resident when its address is outside Canada. CRA asks for T5018 slips for resident subcontractors wherever the work was done, so a resident with a foreign address must have its address corrected. A dedicated non-resident flag is planned.
  • Fees paid to non-residents for services in Canada belong on the T4A-NR, which this module does not produce.
  • Slips show the full SIN to accounting administrators and to the recipient signed in to the portal. Anyone opening a shared link, and read-only accountants, see the last three digits. The CRA file and the emailed PDFs are restricted to accounting administrators.
  • A credit note applied to a bill counts on the day the two are matched, in the time zone set on the company's contact (UTC when none is set). Matching a credit note in a later year moves its effect into that year, so match credit notes before filing.
  • With Odoo Enterprise, a payment recorded without a journal entry that covers both bills and credit notes is split across them by amount, which can be off when their mixes differ. Check those slips' payment lists before filing.
  • No tool yet removes SINs from final or filed returns once the six-year record period ends. Clearing a SIN from the contact leaves the copies on slips and in the stored CRA files.
  • The $500 test looks at box 048 alone. CRA adds up all T4A boxes, so a recipient who gets other T4A amounts from you elsewhere may need a box 048 slip below $500. Do not tag commission accounts: commissions go in box 020.
  • With early payment discounts set to Never on the company, the discount is spread across the bill's tax as well, so box 048 can be a few cents above the fees paid. The Canadian default, On early payment, is exact.
  • A T5018 period that ends on the last day of a month is due on the last day of the sixth month after it, following CRA's rule for corporation returns.
  • Returns always belong to the main company and file under its RP or RZ account. A branch that files under a program account of its own cannot have its own return yet.
